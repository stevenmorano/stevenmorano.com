# StevenMorano.com Personal Website Redesign

Status: Design approved  
Date: July 29, 2026  
Domain: `https://stevenmorano.com`

## 1. Purpose

StevenMorano.com will become Steven Morano's durable personal and professional reputation hub.

The site should help Steven:

- Earn consideration for excellent remote or hybrid marketing operations, marketing manager, and marketing director roles.
- Look established and credible when recruiters, colleagues, friends, family, prospective collaborators, community members, or other people search for his name.
- Build a recognizable online identity that remains useful after a job search ends.
- Demonstrate that he is an experienced marketer with unusually strong technical adaptability, AI fluency, and a habit of building useful systems.
- Give visitors clear paths to his professional profiles, selected work, projects, community, and other websites.

The site is not primarily a consulting funnel, scheduling page, developer portfolio, or exhaustive autobiography.

## 2. Primary Positioning

The central professional story is:

> Steven Morano is a marketing operations leader who makes complex systems work.

Supporting positioning:

- Marketing first.
- Technically fluent.
- Constantly learning and building.
- Experienced in marketing operations, growth, CRM, lifecycle automation, paid acquisition, ecommerce, analytics, customer journeys, and cross-functional leadership.
- Able to learn unfamiliar software quickly and use AI, automation, and lightweight application development to solve practical business problems.

Steven should not be positioned as a traditional software engineer, a full-time agency owner with a large active roster, or an operator of several competing businesses.

## 3. Audience and Desired Impression

The homepage should work for a broad audience without becoming generic.

After a 60- to 90-second visit, someone should understand that Steven:

- Has more than 12 years of legitimate marketing experience.
- Has held director-level responsibility and managed meaningful budgets, teams, systems, and growth programs.
- Is technically capable without pretending to be a world-class programmer.
- Uses AI as practical leverage for marketing, systems, research, automation, and product prototyping.
- Is curious, personable, entrepreneurial, and interested in useful technology.
- Is open to a strong remote or hybrid marketing leadership opportunity.
- Has a wider body of projects, interests, and communities worth exploring.

The site should feel established rather than temporary, needy, overly sales-oriented, or dominated by "open to work" messaging.

## 5. Visual Direction

The approved direction is a dark, modern personal portfolio with technical precision.

Visual principles:

- Near-black and navy page surfaces.
- Cool blue and cyan used as restrained signal colors.
- Modern geometric typography with monospaced interface labels.
- Crisp borders, clear grids, generous spacing, and text-first project cards.
- A real professional portrait in the About section rather than the hero.
- Subtle, purposeful motion that never delays content.
- Strong hierarchy and short reading paths.

Avoid:

- Repeating Smart Marketing Digital's sales-focused structure or animated AI dashboard motifs.
- Glassmorphism-heavy dashboard cards.
- Fake charts, fictional live counters, or decorative business metrics.
- Generic AI-generated imagery.
- Excessive glow effects.
- Long walls of resume content.
- Motion that hides content until scrolling or interferes with screenshots, indexing, or accessibility.

The supplied close-cropped portrait is approved for the About section. A smart-casual portrait may replace it later but should not block launch.

Its fictional "Last 30 Days" dashboard metrics are out of scope for this project and must not be repeated. They should be removed in a separate future project.

## 7. Technical Architecture

The redesign will continue using the existing Next.js application.

Technical principles:

- Static-first rendering.
- Server Components by default.
- Client-side JavaScript only where interaction or motion materially requires it.
- No database, authentication, user accounts, or CMS.
- No contact form at launch; use direct email.
- Manually curated project data.
- Centralized, typed site-content data for low-maintenance editing.
- Local, optimized images using the framework's image component.
- Semantic landmarks, headings, navigation, and links.
- Graceful behavior when JavaScript or animation is unavailable.

The relevant Next.js version documentation under `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` must be consulted before implementation because this repository's Next.js version includes breaking changes.

## 8. Mobile, Accessibility, and Performance

The mobile experience is a first-class requirement, not a compressed desktop layout.

Requirements:

- Clear content priority at narrow widths.
- Large, reliable touch targets.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Readable type and comfortable line lengths.
- Stacked project stories with optimized imagery.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation.
- Visible focus states.
- Sufficient color contrast.
- Skip link and semantic navigation.
- Correct accessible names and expanded-state attributes for the mobile menu.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No essential information conveyed only by animation, color, hover, or imagery.

Target Lighthouse categories are mid-to-high 90s for performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO, recognizing that lab measurements can vary by device and network.

## 9. Search Visibility and Google SEO

The primary achievable SEO goal is to establish StevenMorano.com as the authoritative result for searches involving "Steven Morano" and closely related professional queries. The project will not promise rankings for broad national terms such as "marketing director" or "marketing operations consultant."

### 9.1 On-Site Technical SEO

Implement:

- Descriptive title centered on Steven's name and primary expertise.
- Compelling meta description written for people, not keyword stuffing.
- Canonical URL for the production domain.
- Exact-name H1.
- Crawlable server-rendered text.
- Correct heading hierarchy.
- Descriptive link text.
- Useful image alternative text.
- Valid sitemap and robots output.
- Open Graph and X/Twitter metadata.
- Custom social-sharing image.
- Stable favicon and site icons.
- `ProfilePage`, `Person`, and `WebSite` structured data where valid.
- `sameAs` links connecting LinkedIn, GitHub, X, and other appropriate public profiles.
- Consistent location and professional descriptions.
- Fast page delivery and strong Core Web Vitals.
- No accidental `noindex`, duplicate canonicals, broken internal anchors, or client-only primary content.

### 9.2 Entity and Off-Site Signals

Post-launch work should include:

- Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://stevenmorano.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Request indexing after launch.
- Link to StevenMorano.com consistently from LinkedIn, GitHub, X, Smart Marketing Digital, the Skool profile, and other owned public profiles.
- Use consistent name spelling, portrait, short biography, and domain across profiles.
- Add the site to relevant resume and professional-directory profiles.
- Consider Bing Webmaster Tools for additional discovery.

These owned-profile links are particularly important because they help search engines understand that the domain and profiles represent the same person.

### 9.3 Content Growth

The launch site should stay concise. Future search growth can come from a small number of genuinely useful additions:

- Detailed project pages with original screenshots and explanations.
- Occasional writing about marketing operations, AI-assisted workflows, software learning, or systems building.
- A curated travel or personal story if it supports the broader identity.
- Updates when major projects ship.

Publishing low-value, AI-generated articles solely for keywords is explicitly out of scope.

## 10. Truthfulness, Privacy, and Professional Risk

All public content must be supportable in an interview or professional conversation.

Rules:

- No fictional metrics or mock dashboards presented as real performance.
- Quantified career results must come from legitimate employment experience.
- Smart Marketing Digital must not imply a large active client roster.
- Private projects must be labeled as private or in development.
- AI should be presented as leverage and a working method, not as a substitute for expertise.
- Do not claim Steven is a traditional full-stack engineer or can replace an entire engineering organization.
- Do not publish the eight-page master resume.
- Do not publish a phone number, private Instagram, personal financial details, or family details.
- Do not make five projects look like five operating businesses.

## 11. Maintainability

Content should be easy for Steven or a future coding assistant to update.

Maintain:

- One typed content source for navigation, links, career highlights, proof points, projects, interests, and contact information.
- Clear image naming and project-specific asset folders.
- Honest project-status values from a small controlled list.
- Minimal dependencies and no unnecessary runtime services.
- Documentation that matches the actual implementation.

Automated GitHub feeds are intentionally excluded at launch. Manual curation provides better quality control and prevents unfinished repositories from becoming the site's public story.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is successful when:

- A first-time visitor can identify Steven, his marketing specialty, and his technical differentiator within several seconds.
- The homepage can be meaningfully scanned in 60-90 seconds.
- Job-oriented visitors can reach experience, LinkedIn, GitHub, resume, and email immediately.
- General visitors can understand Steven's projects, interests, and broader identity.
- Home Management is visibly the primary product project and Mindful Eating is second.
- Smart Marketing Digital is clearly secondary.
- The site looks visually distinct from Smart Marketing Digital.
- Mobile navigation and all major sections work comfortably on a phone.
- All public claims, links, and project statuses are accurate.
- Search metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots, and social previews validate correctly.
- Lint and production build complete without errors.
- The deployed page has no console errors, broken links, malformed visuals, or inaccessible essential interactions.
