## Findings

### Finding 1 — Google's indexed snippet still reflects the old consulting positioning

- **Category:** Crawlability & Indexation
- **Evidence:** A July 29, 2026 search surfaced `stevenmorano.com` with the title "Steven Morano | Marketing Operations & AI Systems Consultant" and sales-focused copy from the previous site. The redesign uses a different title, description, and page intent.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Confidence:** High
- **Why it matters:** Until Google recrawls the page, searchers may continue seeing consulting-first language that does not match Steven's current career-focused positioning.
- **Score impact:** −5
- **Recommendation:** Deploy the redesign, submit the sitemap in Google Search Console, inspect the canonical homepage, and request indexing.

### Finding 2 — Post-redesign field performance is not yet measured

- **Category:** Technical Foundations
- **Evidence:** Local tests confirmed a static build, no horizontal overflow, no console errors, and no unnecessary client component boundary. Google Search Console, CrUX, and production analytics were unavailable, so real-user LCP, INP, and CLS cannot yet be verified.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Confidence:** High
- **Why it matters:** Strong implementation signals do not substitute for field data from actual devices and network conditions.
- **Score impact:** −3
- **Recommendation:** Review Core Web Vitals in Search Console and PageSpeed Insights after the redesigned page has enough production traffic.

### Finding 3 — Strong career metrics have limited on-site corroboration

- **Category:** Content Quality & E-E-A-T
- **Evidence:** The homepage presents legitimate outcomes such as $6M in annual media responsibility, 75% lead growth, and 150% enrollment growth, but it does not yet include approved testimonials, recommendations, or deeper case-study context.
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** High
- **Why it matters:** Third-party recommendations or deeper first-hand context would make the strongest claims more persuasive to both people and quality-evaluation systems.
- **Score impact:** −10
- **Recommendation:** Add one or two approved recommendations or concise case studies when credible supporting material is available.

### Finding 4 — The site has little indexable original content beyond the homepage

- **Category:** Authority & Trust Signals
- **Evidence:** The sitemap contains one URL. Project descriptions are substantial enough for the homepage, but there are no indexable project detail pages or original articles.
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** High
- **Why it matters:** A single page can rank for Steven's name, but additional high-quality first-hand material would deepen topical authority and create more surfaces for people to discover and cite.
- **Score impact:** −10
- **Recommendation:** Publish only a small number of genuinely useful project case studies or articles about marketing operations, practical AI, and systems problem-solving.
